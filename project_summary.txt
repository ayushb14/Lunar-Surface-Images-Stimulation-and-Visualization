LUNAR ROVER HAZARD-AWARE NAVIGATION PROJECT
=================================================

This MATLAB project simulates a lunar rover navigating across a cratered surface.
The system creates or loads a lunar terrain image, builds a hazard model, creates
a terrain cost map, runs A* path planning, compares with Dijkstra, and animates
the rover mission.

Main upgrades compared with a basic crater-shortest-path demo:
1. Craters are treated as hazards rather than navigation nodes.
2. Rover path is planned through safe terrain, not through crater centers.
3. Cost map includes roughness, slope, shadow, and hazard clearance.
4. A* is compared with Dijkstra to show algorithmic improvement.
5. 2D, 3D, dashboard, CSV, and animation outputs are generated.
